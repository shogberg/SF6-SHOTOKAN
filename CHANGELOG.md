## 2026-11-25 (MR changes and odds)
- MR per game, from sfbuff's formula (github.com/alanoliveira/sfbuff, `app/models/master_rating_calculator.rb`: Elo with K = 16). `shared_core_db.py` stores `games.p1/p2_mr_change` (observed from the same player's MR in the set's next game, calculated for a set's last game) with `mr_change_source`, and `sets.opp_mr_start`, `focus_mr_end`, `opp_mr_end`; ingest fills them and warns when an observed change disagrees with the formula (usually games stored out of played order). New commands `mr DB [SETKEY ...]` (backfill) and `odds DB MY_MR OPP_MR`. On the author's database the formula matched the next game's MR in 314 of 316 game sides; the other two were one set stored in the wrong order.
- `shared_core_blind_manifest.py` adds `mr_odds` when both players have an MR: each player's MR at stake (win / loss) and game, FT2 and FT3 win chances. Every game has 16 MR at stake, so the MR a player would lose is 16 x their game win chance. replay-review-prediction says to start from it and how far to trust it (small gaps played like coin flips; bigger gaps favoured the higher MR more than the formula says).
- `buckler_replays.py --raw FILE` saves Buckler's raw pageProps for each page fetched, to look for fields the script doesn't read yet. (Checked 2026-11-25: neither the battle log nor the Master ranking page says which ranked phase is current.)

## 2026-11-24 (punish windows)
- New block punish check in `shared_core_analyze.py`: every time a player leaves blockstun while the attacker is still recovering by 5+ frames (measured from the capture), with distance and range (close <= 1.6, mid <= 2.6, far; `PUNISH_CLOSE` / `PUNISH_MID`), and what the defender did (punished, pressed but didn't connect, Drive Parry, jumped, dashed, held / walked). Moves restarted or cancelled during the blockstun are frame traps and skipped. Written to `punish_windows.json` and a "Block punish windows" section in report.md.
- New `tools/frame_data/<char>.json` for 32 characters: SuperCombo wiki Block Adv by input (pulled 2026-11-24), plus `aliases` from analyzer labels to inputs for specials (Ryu, Ken, Akuma, Guile, Sagat, Cammy so far; `?` takes L/M/H from the label, OD doubles the button). Each window is checked: `ok`, `later hit blocked` (multi-hit moves measure less minus than the wiki's first-hit value; still a real window), `wiki disagrees` (left out of counts) or `unverified`.
- sf6.db: new `punish_windows` table; set and game stats `punish_windows_close/mid` and `punishes_landed_close/mid`. Today's 13 ranked sets re-run and re-ingested: .shog had 10 close windows and punished 5 (2 Drive Parries after OD DPs, 2 presses that didn't connect, 1 held). Older sets have no punish data yet.
- quick-review-report reports block punishes; full-review-report adds a missed-punish check and keeping `frame_data` aliases up to date.

## 2026-11-24 (spectra pool)
- Spectra are scored against everyone except the configured player. New `tools/spectra_calibrate.py DB [--min-games 10] [--dry]`: the 20/40/60/80th percentile cut points come from every player-set except the configured player's, each other player weighted equally (min(1, games/10), so players with under 10 games count less), stored in sf6.db (settings `spectra_cuts`); every set is re-scored. `spectra.py` loads them (`load_cuts`) and keeps each score's raw value; `spectra_backfill.py` stores raw values in a new `spectra_raw` table. Run on sf6.db: pool 234 player-sets from 122 players (effective weight 47), 90 of .shog's and 447 other scores moved. .shog's mean Risk tolerance is now 3.7 (everyone else 2.9) and Temperament (offense) 2.8 (3.2).
- New `tools/player_baseline.py DB [--days 7]`: the configured player's set against the median of their own sets in the previous 7 days (spectra raw values with the change on the 1-5 scale, and per-game stats), in a new `player_baseline` table. `shared_core_db.py ingest` runs it.
- New `tools/character_baselines.py DB [--include-player]`: per-character baselines without the configured player, for sf6-character-notes (updated: Cammy now 2.1 forward jumps, 1.9 Drive Rush, 1.5 Drive Parry a round without .shog; his own 2.5, 2.5, 1.1).

## 2026-11-23
- Blind predictions: new `tools/blind_manifest.py` (first game's header only; withholds round results, later games' MR/LP, the number of games and frame data). `sf6db.py predict DB PREDICTION.json` stores a prediction before the analyzer runs in a new `predictions` table, refuses a set already ingested and can't be replaced after ingest; ingest links the prediction to its set and records the grade. `sf6db.py history` returns pre-set history (vs this player, the character matchup, the opponent's reads and habits, your recent leaks) only from sets before `--before`. replay-review-prediction skill copy updated.
- No machine-specific paths in code: new `tools/paths.py` reads `SHOTOKAN_HOME`, `SHOTOKAN_REPLAYS`, `SHOTOKAN_DB`, `SHOTOKAN_CAPTURE_DIRS` and `SHOTOKAN_RANKED_DIR` from the environment or `tools/.env` (see `tools/.env.example`), with defaults relative to the coaching folder. `beh_cache.py`, `ranked_build.py`, `lethal_backfill.py`, `spectra_backfill.py` and `decision_presses.py` use it (captures are found by replay ID as `.json` or `.json.gz`; `decision_presses.py`'s DB now defaults to `SHOTOKAN_DB`). `batch_record.py` takes `SHOTOKAN_HOME` / `SHOTOKAN_REPLAYS` (`REPLAYS_DIR` still works) and no longer defaults to a D: path. README: new Paths section.
- Docs: sf6-meta-description.md "What the data says" rewritten for 122 compared sets (archetypes, what separates winners and what doesn't, decision presses, button economy, prediction accuracy vs the higher-MR baseline) and the winner profile corrected (Initiative even, Resource use 3.9 vs 2.2, Drive Reversal about 2x in losers, wake-up damage not wake-up blocking, Arjun's white health). README: white-health damage, decision presses, spectra at ingest, backfill and rescore tools, Economy of buttons. GETTING-STARTED and TODO updated; skill copies (sf6-replay-analysis, sf6-coach-shog) match the live skills.
- Decision presses: a throw now counts as landed when the opponent is held, not only when its damage arrives inside the ~70-frame window. Long throws (Juri's) were all counted as whiffs before.
- Spectra on every ingest: `sf6db.py ingest` now computes the eight player spectra for the set it just ingested (spectra table, and attached to the review's hand-written archetypes), adding the set's replays to `beh_cache.json` first so Composure, Initiative, Resource use and Temperament (offense) aren't left blank. `--no-spectra` skips it. New `tools/spectra_backfill.py DB [SET_KEY ...]` fills sets that have none (until now only the batch ingest scripts computed spectra, so a plain ingest left them empty).
- Decision presses in detail: analyze_capture.py now returns each decision press with its situation (air button, vs a jump, wake-up, after blocking, own pressure, oki, after a hit, or neutral close / mid / far), the move, what the opponent was doing, and the first outcome within about 70 frames (landed, blocked, parried, whiffed, got hit, whiff punished). New `tools/decision_presses.py DB --player NAME[,ALIAS] [--char C]` compares players: presses per round by situation, outcome rates, and the top moves per situation with how often each landed or got them hit (throw presses on wake-up or after blocking are reported as throw techs).
- Damage now counts white (recoverable) health. A hit's damage is permanent life lost: the drop in `hp_recoverable_cap` over the combo, including any white health the hit cashed in; a hit that only adds white health (Arjun's Indomitable Defense counter, Drive Reversal) counts 0. New hit fields `recoverable` (white health the combo added) and `white_cashed` (white health it wiped out), also stored in sf6db.py's `hits` table. On a C.Viper set, lost rounds that didn't add up to a full life bar went from 10 of 23 to 2; the old count ran about 5% low.
- Damage is read from the frame before the first hit (some moves leave the victim in a damage state with no hitstun, so the old lookback read HP after the hit), runs on while the victim is still being hit or held (cinematic supers log their last hits late), and never reaches back into the previous combo on the same victim (no more double counting).
- Command grabs whose hits land long after the Throw stat (Arjun's Ghastly Interrogation, about 145 frames) are no longer counted twice, and are tagged as throws.
- Arjun's moves are named: Arm of Justice (+ follow-up), Breathing Ring / Breathing Shockwave, Indomitable Defense (stance, counter) and Moral Breakdown, Divine Pillar, Cruel Shoulder and Justice Beyond, Ghastly Interrogation, SA1 Howling Destruction, SA2 Instant Oblivion, SA3 Yaksha's Order. Divine Pillar (`03`) and Cruel Shoulder (`623P`) are inferred from how the moves behave. Hits from a pillar or ring set up earlier are credited to that move instead of Arjun's walk or jump.
- `tools/rescore_damage.py`: re-scores damage in an existing history database from the captures, without re-ingesting (reads, habits, review docs and press counts are kept), and recomputes the damage-based set_stats and game_stats.
