# Guideline skills: what each one checks

Part of the sf6-replay-analysis skill (the list the core loads every review). Read when the skill points here.

**Guideline skills to load every review.** Before analysing any replay, load each of these skills with the Skill tool and apply them alongside this one:
- `sf6-the-defense-equation-exdojo`: judging defensive decisions (risk/reward vs the take-the-throw baseline, the six modifiers, frequency and conditioning, "could you have guessed better?").
- `sf6-improving-shimmy-exdojo`: judging throw and shimmy offense (lab shimmies, react instead of autopiloting, commit, gather throw-tech info before shimmying, in-and-out shimmy and delay-jab checks), and the opponent's throw defence.
- `sf6-stagger-pressure-exdojo`: classify each player's defensive timing (reversal, quick ~3f, safe ~6f, reactive ~12f), and judge frame traps vs staggers, manual vs automatic staggers, checkpoints, out-of-range jab staggers and defence after a failed stagger.
- `sf6-player-archetypes-exdojo`: assign each player (Sean and the opponent) an archetype (Aggressive, Defensive, Fundamentalist, Balanced, with a lean) and a confidence level (High / Medium / Low) with timestamped evidence, and give the matching counter. Include this in every report; it's interpretive, so state the confidence rather than overclaiming.
- `sf6-structuring-offense-defense-exdojo`: tag oki/pressure options as immediate or delayed, identify each player's primary and panic defensive options and the message they send, and name the Venn-diagram answer to the opponent's shown option set.
- `sf6-consistency-exdojo`: judge each player's game plan and win condition, round-start choices, and volatile decisions (fireballs in jump range, whiffed heavies, committal specials, random turn-steals), plus safe-jump use.
- `sf6-how-to-jump-exdojo`: log jumps by range and button timing, umbrella anti-air blind spots, jumps used as whiff punishes, offense-to-neutral jump traps, and delayed cross-ups.
- `sf6-jab-strings-exdojo`: log light strings (chain gaps, enders, spacing), the defender's habits during and after strings (mash, delay tech, delay jab, after-string button), and whether the attacker adapted.
- `sf6-footsies-101-exdojo`: for neutral exchanges, note the zone change before each hit (green/yellow/red, backdash, after a tech) and the button it triggered, classify it by the footsies triangle (poke, whiff punish, advance), and flag red-zone guesses, wasted movement and missed whiff punishes.
- `sf6-spacing-traps-exdojo`: after blocked strings and movement triggers, log each player's spacing responses (2MK, jump, Drive Rush, backdash, walk-in), the gap, and whether the other player had a counter ready. Treat failed traps as information.
- `sf6-defense-offense-101-exdojo`: default to blocking when ahead on health and Drive, look for manipulation chains (show an option, then switch once they adapt), check reversal baiting on offense and "play the player, not the character", and end the defense section with one focus for Sean's next session.
- `sf6-air-footsies-exdojo`: log anti-air attempts by range and tool (umbrella vs far-range), flag green-zone jumps that went unanswered or were answered with the wrong tool, and spot habitual umbrella anti-airs that empty jumps or late air normals can whiff punish.

- `sf6-dps-and-divekicks-exdojo`: log every dive kick (height, advantage, instant vs apex) and the defender's answer; check Sean's Cammy dive-kick spacing and follow-ups, missed punishes on minus dive kicks, and DPs baited by short dive kicks.
- `sf6-holistic-lens-exdojo`: at key decisions, read screen position, both health bars, Drive and super together to say what each player likely wanted (corner escapes, wake-up DP or parry), and flag Drive spent for small damage.
- `sf6-drive-parry-mindgames-exdojo`: log every Drive Parry (situation, outcome, Drive before/after), flag parries that got thrown, note repeated target combos to perfect parry, and check Sean's plan against very aggressive players.
- `sf6-dealing-with-drive-rush-exdojo`: for each opponent Drive Rush, log the range, Sean's answer (light, medium, parry, block) and result; flag standing in the hot spot and checks that whiffed or lost where parry would have won.
- `sf6-drive-rush-blockstrings-exdojo`: for each button~Drive Rush, note true vs gapped follow-up and the defender's response (DPOS, mash, block, thrown); flag gapped strings Sean could have DPOS'd and DPOS attempts into baiting enders.
- `sf6-delay-option-selects-exdojo`: classify each oki/pressure setup as delay-tech territory (strike and throw both tight) or delay jump-back / delay jab territory (throw needs a walk-up), judge Sean's timing, and flag option selects where blocking was better.
- `sf6-drive-reversal-exdojo`: list blocked opponent moves that were Drive Reversal-able (~21+ frames after contact) and led to bad situations, flag baited wake-up Drive Reversals (-6), note refunded wake-up Drive Reversals vs throws, and resource trades.
- `sf6-back-recovery-exdojo`: for each knockdown, note back roll vs normal recovery and what the attacker did next; flag throw loops a back roll would break and back rolls that helped zoners.
- `sf6-beating-unstable-players-exdojo`: vs unstable or random opponents, check full punishes on unsafe options, the opponent's "inevitable tools" and Sean's answers, round openers vs gambit characters, and skill/knowledge checks.
- `sf6-matchups-gameplans-exdojo`: state each character's game plan from its kit, who controlled spacing relative to the key pokes, the punish relationships that mattered (and missed ones), and whether Sean adjusted beyond layer 1.
- `sf6-using-pokes-exdojo`: identify each player's main poke and range, whether it outranged the other's cancelable 2MK and pushed out of 2MK range, and what the opponent did after Sean's pokes.
- `sf6-hit-confirms-exdojo`: flag meter dumped on block and dropped confirms on hit (confirm off the Drive Rush normal, not off 2MK), Drive spent on unconfirmed cancels before burnout, and throw attempts made without holding forward.
- `sf6-simple-gameplan-exdojo`: write Sean's Cammy plan as it showed (control tools, gap-checked Drive Rush string, combos, oki per knockdown, throw/shimmy spots), note what's missing, and label the opponent's reactions as habit vs choice.
- `sf6-delayed-throw-offense-exdojo`: when Sean shimmies or down-backs to bait a reversal, check whether a 7-9f delayed forward throw would have kept his turn; spot naturally delayed throws (beat supers, lose to ODP), back throws that gave up the corner, and delays punished by wake-up neutral jump or big buttons.
- `sf6-reactions-exdojo`: for hits from Drive Rush, Drive Impact, jumps and whiffs, separate reaction problems from positioning/anticipation problems; log patterns each player shows, judge answers by mental-stack cost and risk (super/perfect parry vs Drive Impact, down-back vs 2MK~Drive Rush), and flag defensive-option overuse.

- `sf6-coach-doctaafrikan`: Sean's own coaching from DoctaAfrikan; weight it heavily. Categorize the opponent by risk tolerance and temperament, pop-quiz their "definitely comes up" moves and two likely follow-ups (tier 1/2/3 Venn answers), flag boogeyman-fighting, check that Sean's offense has an info-gathering default (exits when the string is done, throws enough, throws fuzzy-jabbers), check whether he plays Cammy as a scam artist (dive kicks as whiff punishes at 2MK range), and list habits he could have cashed out on.
- `sf6-coach-coosco`: Coosco's replay breakdowns (two of the first three are Cammy). Check walk-forward vs stationary or jump approaches after the opponent "knocks on the door", turtling vs strategic patience, the clue log (what the opponent does after dropped combos, low-reward hits and missed whiff punishes, and what Sean does after his own frustrating moments), turns given up (throws after minus lights midscreen, point-blank unsafe normals, Drive Rush 2LK after a throw), Cammy's walk-forward after drills and round-start Drive Rush, corner suffocation, and committal tools (DI, wake-up supers, round-start hard reads).

- `sf6-coach-shog`: Sean's own coaching rules, which override the other skills where they conflict: cancelable 2MK can't be hit-confirmed (non-cancelable +4 on hit can), classify wake-up options (normal = read, invincible reversal = decision, unsafe non-invincible special = input error), and don't say "tech more" by default (only tech a shown pattern; a taken throw is often the safe choice).

- `sf6-character-notes`: character baselines (jumps, Drive Rush, parries, throws per round) and notes, so a habit is judged against what is normal for that character (a Cammy jumping a lot is expected; a Ryu jumping a lot is likely a leak). Suggest new notes when a review shows something character-specific.

- `sf6-footsies-handbook-maj`: Maj's Footsies Handbook (Elements 01-35, Rules #1-#4, projectiles), with SF6 translations. Check neutral baits, the corner, super/DI baiting, "what was this jump meant to land on?", walk-in strike/throw timing, crossup setups, predictability and tactic vs gimmick; cite elements by number. Its examples come from older games: when an SF6 translation proves wrong, update that line.
- `sf6-fgc-glossary` (reference, load when needed): Infil's FGC glossary (General + Street Fighter terms) for consistent wording and quick definitions.

- `sf6-drive-efficiency`: value every Drive and super spend against the best free route (damage, knockdown advantage, corner, Drive damage dealt, Drive gained back) on offense, and against blocking (chip, Drive lost blocking, the attacker's shown follow-up frequencies) on defense; each player's Drive economy (spent, lost blocking, lost to punish counters / Drive Impact, gained from hits, blocks and parries); parry net Drive and times thrown out of parry; what drained each player before a burnout.

When Sean adds another guideline skill, add it to this list.
